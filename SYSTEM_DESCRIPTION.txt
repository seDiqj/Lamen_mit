================================================================================
INTEGRATED MICROFINANCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Microfinance · Accounting · HRMIS  —  Full System Description
================================================================================


PART 1.  SYSTEM AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This platform is an integrated, web-based management system for a
microfinance institution. It brings together three major business areas
into a single application with a single database, a single login, and a
unified user experience:

  1) MICROFINANCE   — Customer, loan and portfolio management
  2) ACCOUNTING     — Full double-entry bookkeeping and financial reporting
  3) HRMIS          — Human Resources & Management Information System

Each module is fully featured on its own, but the real value comes from
how tightly they are integrated: a single business event (a loan
disbursement, a collection, a salary payment) automatically flows
through to every relevant module — operational records, accounting
journals, dashboards, alerts and reports.

Cross-cutting capabilities shared by all three modules:

  • Multi-branch with central head-office consolidation
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) with page-level permissions
  • Authentication via secure single sign-on (OpenID Connect)
  • Activity log / audit trail (who did what, when, from which IP)
  • Bilingual support (English + Persian/Dari) and RTL layout
  • Document attachments via secure object storage
  • Excel and PDF export across all major reports
  • Custom Report Builder for ad-hoc reporting on any data source


================================================================================
PART 2.  MICROFINANCE MODULE
================================================================================

2.1  PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The microfinance module manages the full life-cycle of customers and
financing contracts — from initial customer registration, through loan
application and approval, disbursement, repayment collection, restructuring,
and closure, all the way to portfolio analytics and regulatory reporting.

2.2  CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Customer master file with personal, contact and demographic data
  • Linked tables for businesses, business licenses and supporting
    documents
  • Integrated province / district lookup for accurate addressing
  • Document attachments (ID copies, photos, business licenses,
    contracts, etc.)
  • License-expiry tracking with dashboard alerts before expiry
  • Customer search, filter, and history view

2.3  LOAN ORIGINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Loan / financing application form supporting Islamic-finance
    products (Murabaha, Mudaraba, Ijara, Salam, Istisna, etc.) as
    well as conventional products
  • Configurable financing products with cycle-based limits
    (each cycle has its own minimum and maximum amount)
  • Sector / business / financing-purpose / collateral-type lookups
  • Multi-collateral support per loan
  • Multiple guarantors per loan (financial and family guarantors)
  • Funding-source tagging on every loan (track which fund / donor
    money was used)

2.4  APPROVAL WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A configurable, multi-stage approval workflow is built in:

   pending
     ↓
   data quality review     (FAD review)
     ↓
   risk & compliance review
     ↓
   committee review        (multi-vote committee)
     ↓
   approved   →  disbursed  →  active  →  completed
     ↘ rejected / returned

Each stage is permission-controlled. Reviewers add notes, vote, and
return loans for clarification. The full approval history is preserved.

2.5  DISBURSEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Single and bulk disbursement
  • Automatic generation of the installment schedule
      - Standard products → monthly installment plan
      - Mudaraba / profit-sharing products → single bullet
        installment at maturity
  • Automatic posting of the disbursement journal entry
    (financing receivable / cash-or-bank), with the receivable
    account taken from the financing product

2.6  REPAYMENTS & COLLECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Installment register (per-loan repayment schedule)
  • Cash collection, bank collection and partial-payment support
  • Collection approval workflow (pending → approved / rejected)
  • Automatic journal entries for principal, profit and any
    write-offs / fees on approval
  • Bulk collection processing
  • Loan transfers (re-assigning a loan from one finance officer to
    another)

2.7  PORTFOLIO MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Real-time portfolio outstanding, total collection, profit booked
  • PAR (Portfolio at Risk) categories and PAR report
  • Loan classification (standard / watch / sub-standard / doubtful /
    loss) for provisioning
  • Active customer outstanding report
  • Monthly due summary
  • Disbursement targets and achievement tracking
  • Profitability analysis (per branch, officer, product, sector)

2.8  REPORTS (Operational)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Loan disbursement report, contract data report, collateral report,
  individual report, citizen balance statement, shareholder report,
  installment management, monthly due summary, PAR report, profitability
  analysis, LCTR report, subject-role report, custom-built reports via
  the Custom Report Builder, and many more.

2.9  REGULATORY (DAB) REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A dedicated regulatory reporting suite produces the formal statements
required by the central bank (Da Afghanistan Bank), with the same
five tabs an examiner expects:

  1. Notes to Financial Statements        (account-based, as-of date)
  2. Statement of Profit or Loss          (P&L with code mapping)
  3. Statement of Cash Flow               (operating / financing /
                                           investing activities)
  4. Statement of Financial Position      (balance sheet with
                                           Restricted / Unrestricted /
                                           Total columns)
  5. Statement of Changes in Equity       (Share Capital, Retained
                                           Earnings, Dividends,
                                           Revaluation Reserve)

These are produced directly from the live accounting data — no
manual re-keying.


================================================================================
PART 3.  ACCOUNTING MODULE
================================================================================

3.1  PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A complete double-entry bookkeeping engine sits underneath the
microfinance and HR modules. Every business event that has financial
consequences is converted into balanced journal entries, posted to a
configurable chart of accounts, and consolidated into financial
statements that comply with both general accounting principles and the
central bank's regulatory framework.

3.2  CHART OF ACCOUNTS (CoA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Hierarchical (parent / child) accounts with unlimited depth
  • Five primary account types:
       Asset   Liability   Equity   Income   Expense
  • Detailed sub-types for richer reporting and DAB classification:
       Current Asset, Non-Current Asset, Fixed Asset
       Current Liability, Non-Current Liability
       Owner's Capital
       Operating Income, Non-Operating Income, Other Income
       Operating Expense, Non-Operating Expense, Cost of Financing
  • Each account has:
       account code (unique), account name, normal balance
       (debit/credit), opening balance, current balance,
       active/inactive flag, system-account flag
  • Bulk import of CoA from spreadsheet
  • One-click recalculation of all account balances from posted
    journal entries

3.3  FISCAL PERIODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Fiscal year and 12 monthly periods
  • Period close: once a period is closed, no further entries can be
    posted to it (admin only)
  • Each journal entry is automatically tagged to a fiscal period

3.4  JOURNAL ENTRIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Header + lines structure (one entry, many debit/credit lines)
  • Strict double-entry validation: total debits MUST equal total
    credits; minimum two lines; no negative amounts
  • Lifecycle: draft → posted → (optionally) reversed
  • Posting locks the entry; only admins may un-post
  • Reversal generates a mirror entry referencing the original; the
    original is flagged isReversed
  • Every line carries:
       account, description, debit, credit, optional
       funding-source tag (so multi-fund accounting is possible)
  • Each entry can carry a reference to the source business event
    (loan ID, disbursement ID, collection ID, payroll run ID,
    benefit enrolment, manual entry…) so you can drill from the
    statement back to the originating transaction.

3.5  AUTOMATIC JOURNAL ENTRIES (INTEGRATION POINTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The accounting module is fed automatically by the operational modules.
Examples:

  Loan disbursement              Dr Financing Receivable
                                 Cr Cash / Bank

  Collection approved            Dr Cash / Bank
                                 Cr Financing Receivable (principal)
                                 Cr Profit / Margin Income

  Loan write-off / provision     Dr Loan Loss Expense
                                 Cr Provision for Loan Losses

  Payroll run finalised          Dr Salary Expense
                                 Dr Allowance Expense
                                 Cr Statutory Payables (tax, etc.)
                                 Cr Net Salary Payable / Bank

  Benefit cost                   Dr Employee Benefits Expense
                                 Cr Benefits Payable / Bank

The receivable account used for disbursement is configurable per
financing product (with a sensible default fallback), so different
products can be tracked on different GL accounts. Each entry is fully
reversible to handle errors cleanly.

3.6  FINANCIAL REPORTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Trial Balance (date-filtered, posted-only)
  • General Ledger / Account Statement (all activity for one account
    in a period, with running balance)
  • Income Statement (P&L) — handles all income/expense sub-types
    with correct sign treatment (operating income, non-operating
    income, other income, operating expense, non-operating expense,
    cost of financing)
  • Balance Sheet
  • Cash Flow Statement
  • Profit & Loss statement report (export-ready)
  • DAB Regulatory Report set (5 tabs, see §2.9)

3.7  ACCOUNTING DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A dedicated accounting dashboard surfaces:
  • Total posted entries and posting rate
  • Current period status (open / closed)
  • Cash & bank position
  • Top expense / income accounts
  • Recent journal entries
  • Out-of-balance detector

3.8  CONTROLS & GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Role-restricted access — financial reports are limited to
    manager/admin
  • Page-level permissions on chart-of-accounts, journals, and
    each statement
  • Full audit trail — every post, un-post, reverse and edit is
    logged with user, time and IP
  • Server-side enforcement of branch isolation on every accounting
    query (you only see your branch unless you're a head-office user)

PART 4.  HRMIS MODULE
================================================================================

4.1  PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The HRMIS (Human Resources Management Information System) covers the
complete employee life-cycle — from recruitment, onboarding, day-to-day
HR operations (attendance, leave, payroll, benefits) through to
performance management, training and exit.

4.2  ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Departments — hierarchical, each with an optional manager
  • Positions / Job Titles — belong to a department, support both a
    primary reporting line (parentPosition) and a secondary /
    matrix reporting line
  • Visual organisation chart combining departments and positions

4.3  EMPLOYEE MASTER DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A digital staff file modelled on the standard Staff Personal
Information Form:

  Identification:    employee code, link to system user account,
                     link to finance officer record
  Personal:          first/last name, father's name, gender, date
                     of birth, age, marital status
  National ID:       NIC / Tazkira number and place of issue
  Contact:           phone(s), email, permanent & current address
  Employment:        position, department, branch, duty station,
                     hire date, termination date, employment status
                     (active / on_leave / suspended / terminated /
                     resigned)
  Education:         level (not_graduate / high_school / bachelor /
                     master / phd) and details
  Experience:        total, job-related and other years
  Photo:             stored in secure object storage

Related digital file:
  • Emergency contacts
  • Languages spoken with proficiency
  • Family members
  • Professional references
  • Documents (contracts, ID copies, certificates) with expiry
    tracking

4.4  ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Daily attendance per employee
  • Check-in / check-out timestamps, work hours and overtime hours
  • Status: present, absent, late, half-day, on-leave, holiday
  • Approved leaves and public holidays are reflected automatically
  • Drives payroll proration

4.5  LEAVE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Leave types (paid / unpaid, days per year, carry-forward rules)
  • Per-employee, per-year leave balances (entitlement, used,
    carried-forward, remaining)
  • Leave request workflow:
       pending → approved / rejected / cancelled
    with approver, approval timestamp and rejection reason
  • Approval automatically deducts from the leave balance
  • Holiday calendar (recurring and non-recurring)

4.6  PAYROLL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Salary structures (named pay scales, base salary, currency)
  • Allowance types (fixed or percentage, taxable / non-taxable)
  • Deduction types (fixed or percentage, statutory / non-statutory)
  • Effective-dated employee compensation:
       - Employee salary history (promotions / revisions)
       - Employee allowances
       - Employee deductions
  • Payroll runs:
       payroll number, period start/end, payment date,
       totals (gross, deductions, net),
       status workflow: draft → pending_approval → approved → paid
       → cancelled
  • Per-employee payroll details and payslips
  • Automatic accounting journal on finalisation (see §3.5)

4.7  RECRUITMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Job postings (title, position, department, employment type,
    salary range, openings, status: draft / published / closed,
    publish & closing dates)
  • Applicants:
       contact info, resume / cover letter (object storage),
       LinkedIn, current company & position, expected salary,
       experience, applicant status, rating, source
  • Interviews:
       per applicant, interviewer is an employee, type
       (phone / video / in-person / technical), schedule, duration,
       location, status (scheduled / completed / cancelled / no_show),
       feedback, rating, recommendation (hire / no-hire / maybe)
  • End-to-end pipeline from posting to onboarding into the
    employees table

4.8  PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Performance periods (e.g. H1, H2, annual)
  • Performance reviews:
       self / manager / overall ratings (1–5), comments, strengths,
       improvement areas, status workflow:
       draft → self_review → manager_review → completed → acknowledged
  • Goals / objectives:
       per employee, optionally tied to a review,
       weight, progress, status (not_started → in_progress →
       completed / deferred / cancelled)
  • Competency library and competency ratings per review
       (core vs role-specific competencies)

4.9  TRAINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Training programs catalogue
  • Training sessions (scheduled instances of a program)
  • Training enrolments per employee
  • Status tracking: planned → in_progress → completed / cancelled

4.10 BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Benefit plans by type:
       health, dental, vision, life, retirement, transportation,
       housing, food, other
  • Provider, coverage details, employer & employee contributions
    (fixed or percentage), effective and termination dates
  • Employee enrolment with coverage level (employee /
    employee+spouse / family) and dependent count
  • Dependents register (name, relationship, DOB, national ID)

4.11 HR DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real-time analytics on headcount, attendance, leave utilisation,
recruitment funnel, payroll totals, performance review completion and
training participation.

PART 5.  CROSS-MODULE INTEGRATION
================================================================================

The three modules are not bolted-on silos — they share the same
database, the same authentication, the same branch and role model, and
the same audit log. Below are the key integration paths.

5.1  IDENTITY & ACCESS  (one user, one login, three modules)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • A single user account (managed via secure single sign-on) is used
    across Microfinance, Accounting and HRMIS.
  • A user can be linked to:
       - a finance officer record (microfinance)
       - an employee record (HR)
    so the same person is recognised across all three areas without
    duplicate data entry.
  • Roles (admin, manager, CFO, COO, CEO, FAD, Risk & Compliance,
    Sharia, Finance Officer, User…) drive what each user can see and
    do in every module.
  • Page-level permissions can be granted directly to a user OR
    inherited from the user's role.

5.2  BRANCH ISOLATION  (multi-branch with HQ consolidation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • All three modules respect a central branch context.
  • A branch-locked user (e.g. a branch officer) only ever sees their
    own branch's customers, loans, journals and employees — enforced
    on the server, not just hidden in the UI.
  • Head-office users can switch branch from the global selector or
    consolidate across branches.

5.3  MICROFINANCE → ACCOUNTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every operational microfinance event automatically generates the
correct accounting journal entry:

  • Loan disbursement
       Dr Financing Receivable (account configured per product)
       Cr Cash / Bank
  • Collection approved
       Dr Cash / Bank
       Cr Financing Receivable (principal)
       Cr Profit / Margin Income
  • Loan write-off / provisioning
       Dr Loan Loss Expense
       Cr Provision for Loan Losses
  • Reversal of any of the above creates a mirror entry, preserving
    the audit trail.

The journal entry stores a reference back to the source loan,
disbursement or collection, so an auditor can drill from the financial
statement straight back to the originating microfinance transaction.

5.4  HRMIS → ACCOUNTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HR transactions also flow into the GL automatically:

  • Payroll run finalised
       Dr Salary Expense  /  Dr Allowance Expense
       Cr Statutory Payables  /  Cr Net Salary Payable / Bank
  • Benefit costs
       Dr Employee Benefits Expense
       Cr Benefits Payable / Bank
  • Each posting is reversible and tagged with its source HR record.

5.5  HRMIS ↔ MICROFINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • A finance officer in the microfinance module IS an employee in
    HR — same person, two business roles, one record.
  • Loan officers' branches and statuses are read from HR, so
    departures and transfers are reflected immediately in the loan
    portfolio.
  • Performance reviews can use real microfinance KPIs (disbursement
    targets, PAR, profitability) as inputs.

5.6  REPORTING & AUDIT  (one source of truth)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Operational reports (microfinance), financial statements
    (accounting) and HR analytics all draw from the same database
    in real time — no spreadsheets, no re-keying.
  • The activity log records every meaningful action (create, update,
    delete, post, approve, disburse, collect, payroll-run, etc.)
    with user, IP and timestamp.
  • The Custom Report Builder lets authorised users design their own
    reports across customers, loans, installments, collections,
    guarantors, disbursements (and is extensible to HR and accounting
    sources), then save them as shared templates and export to Excel
    or PDF.

PART 6.  TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

6.1  HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Client (browser, desktop or mobile-friendly web UI)
            │     HTTPS, JSON
            ▼
  Application server  (Node.js / Express / TypeScript)
            │     SQL (parameterised, typed via ORM)
            ▼
  PostgreSQL  (single relational database, ~80 tables)
            ⇅
  Object Storage  (documents, photos, resumes, contracts)
            ⇅
  Identity Provider  (OpenID Connect — secure single sign-on)

A single deployment serves all three modules. There are no separate
servers for HR, Microfinance and Accounting — they share runtime,
database and security boundary.

6.2  FRONTEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • React 18 with TypeScript (strict typing front-to-back)
  • Wouter for lightweight client-side routing
  • TanStack React Query for server state, caching and revalidation
  • shadcn/ui component library on top of Radix UI primitives
  • Tailwind CSS with CSS variables; light/dark theme support
  • React Hook Form + Zod for form validation (the same Zod schemas
    are reused on the backend)
  • Recharts for dashboard and report visualisations
  • Bilingual (English / Persian-Dari) with right-to-left layout
  • Excel export via SheetJS / similar; PDF export via server- and
    client-side renderers depending on the report

6.3  BACKEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Express.js on Node.js with TypeScript
  • RESTful JSON API
  • Drizzle ORM for type-safe SQL against PostgreSQL
  • drizzle-zod to derive Zod validators from table definitions, so
    the database schema, the API contract and the frontend forms
    all share one source of truth
  • Passport.js for authentication strategies
  • express-session with a PostgreSQL session store
    (connect-pg-simple) — sessions survive restarts, 30-minute
    rolling idle timeout
  • Bundled for production with esbuild

6.4  DATA LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • PostgreSQL is the single source of truth for all three modules
  • Approximately 80 tables grouped by domain:
       - Auth & RBAC: users, sessions, user_roles, lookup_roles,
         page_permissions, role_page_permissions
       - Org / Lookup: branches, finance_officers, sectors,
         businesses, provinces, districts, license_types,
         financing_purposes, collateral_types, client_occupations,
         funding_sources, financing_products
       - Microfinance: customers, customer_documents,
         customer_businesses, business_licenses, loans, collaterals,
         guarantors, loan_approvals, fad_reviews,
         risk_compliance_reviews, committee_votes, disbursements,
         installments, par_categories, loan_transfers,
         collection_records, disbursement_targets
       - Accounting: accounts, fiscal_periods, journal_entries,
         journal_lines
       - HRMIS: departments, positions, employees,
         employee_emergency_contacts, employee_languages,
         employee_family_members, employee_references,
         employee_documents, leave_types, leave_balances,
         leave_requests, holidays, attendance, salary_structures,
         allowance_types, deduction_types, employee_salaries,
         employee_allowances, employee_deductions, payroll_runs
         (and detail rows), job_postings, applicants, interviews,
         performance_periods, performance_reviews,
         performance_goals, competencies, competency_ratings,
         training_programs, training_sessions, training_enrollments,
         benefit_plans, employee_benefit_enrollments,
         benefit_dependents
       - Cross-cutting: notifications, activity_logs

  • Schema migrations managed via Drizzle Kit (`db:push`).
  • All foreign keys are explicit; status fields use PostgreSQL
    enums where appropriate.

6.5  SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Authentication via OpenID Connect (single sign-on); no
    application-side password storage
  • Session cookies are HTTP-only and server-side; rolling 30-minute
    idle timeout
  • Two-layer authorisation:
       1. Direct role value (e.g. cfo, finance_officer, admin)
       2. Role TYPE from the lookup_roles table (user / managerial /
          admin) — both checked on every protected route
  • Page-level permissions evaluated server-side on every request
  • Branch isolation is enforced by the server, not the client:
    a helper extracts the "effective branch" for the request and
    overrides any client-side branchId for branch-locked users
  • Strict double-entry validation in accounting (debits = credits,
    no negatives, minimum two lines)
  • Closed fiscal periods cannot accept new entries
  • All write operations are validated against Zod schemas before
    reaching the database
  • Activity log captures user, IP, action, entity, entity ID,
    description and timestamp on every significant operation
  • Object storage uses scoped, authenticated URLs

6.6  PROJECT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  client/src/        React frontend
    components/      shadcn/ui components
    hooks/           Custom React hooks
    lib/             Query client, utilities
    pages/           Route pages (dashboards, lists, forms, reports)
      hr/            HRMIS pages
  server/            Express backend
    replit_integrations/auth/  Authentication setup
    routes.ts        API route definitions
    storage.ts       Database operations (storage interface)
  shared/            Code shared between frontend & backend
    schema.ts        Drizzle schema (single source of truth for data)
    models/          Shared type definitions
  migrations/        Drizzle migration files

6.7  BUILD, RUN & DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Development: a single command starts both the API and the Vite
    dev server (proxied through Express, hot-module reload).
  • Production: the frontend is bundled by Vite into static assets;
    the backend is bundled by esbuild into a single CommonJS server.
  • A single Node.js process serves the bundled frontend and the API
    on the same port behind HTTPS.
  • Runtime requirements: Node.js, PostgreSQL, an object-storage
    bucket, and an OpenID Connect identity provider.
  • Environment configuration via environment variables / secrets
    (database URL, session secret, OIDC issuer URL, identifiers,
    object storage paths). Secrets are never stored in source.

6.8  OBSERVABILITY & GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • In-app activity log accessible to admins / auditors
  • Dashboards across all three modules with real-time data
  • Notifications table for in-app alerts
  • License-expiry, due-installment and collection alerts surfaced
    on the main dashboard with click-through to the underlying
    record

PART 7.  SUMMARY
================================================================================

This system delivers what a microfinance institution actually needs:

  • A complete MICROFINANCE engine that runs the loan business —
    customers, applications, multi-stage approvals, disbursements,
    collections, restructuring, portfolio analytics and central-bank
    reporting.

  • A full ACCOUNTING engine that turns every operational event into
    proper double-entry journals, posts them to a configurable chart
    of accounts, controls fiscal periods, and produces every
    standard financial statement plus the regulatory DAB report set.

  • A full HRMIS that manages the workforce that runs the business —
    organisation structure, employee files, attendance, leave,
    payroll, benefits, recruitment, performance and training.

  • A tight INTEGRATION layer so the three behave as one system:
    one login, one branch model, one role model, one audit trail,
    one source of truth — with operational events flowing
    automatically into accounting and HR data flowing into payroll
    journals.

  • A modern, secure TECHNICAL FOUNDATION (TypeScript end-to-end,
    PostgreSQL, OpenID Connect, RBAC, server-enforced branch
    isolation, validated APIs, full audit log) that is ready for
    growth and for regulatory scrutiny.
